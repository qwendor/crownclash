HOW TO PLAY

ELIXIR refills on its own, up to 10. Every card costs elixir, so spend it like money: trade up, never dump your whole bar at once.

DEPLOY by dragging a card onto your half of the arena. Wreck an enemy tower and your deploy zone pushes up that lane.

TOWERS Three a side. Each one you destroy is a crown. Most crowns at time wins, and taking the King Tower wins instantly.

CLOCK Elixir flows double for the last minute. A tie at zero goes to overtime: triple elixir, first tower to fall wins.

TARGETING Some troops only swing at ground units, some chase buildings and walk right past your defenders. Read the card before you drop it.
